The Evolution and Technology of Snus Packing Machines

Snus, a smokeless tobacco product originating from Sweden, has grown in popularity worldwide due to its convenience and the perception of it being a less harmful alternative to smoking. The demand for snus has led to significant advancements in snus packing machines, which are critical for ensuring product quality, consistency, and efficiency in production. This article explores the technology behind snus packing machines, their evolution, types, working principles, and future trends.

Despite market watchers predicting a crisis as early as 2021, and forecasting a price crash in 2022, and making similar predictions in 2023, Dubai defied all pessimistic expectations — 2024 has broken all records.

In the third quarter of 2024, Dubai's real estate transactions reached 141 billion AED ($38.6 billion). This marks a 30% increase compared to the same period in 2023.

Prices continue to rise, but as with any investment, understanding the details is crucial — much depends on the specific property.

  • Lower-priced properties carry more risk. For example, a studio apartment in a residential area that's indistinguishable from hundreds of similar units could see a significant price drop. That said, rental rates are rising rapidly, which makes these properties well suited for passive income from short- and long-term rentals.
  • Premium properties are more secure and show more stable price growth. A penthouse in Business Bay or a townhouse in Dubai Hills Estate is likely to appreciate in value. The same applies to apartments in these areas.

Why Are Prices Rising

Here are 6 key reasons behind Dubai's real estate price growth:

1. Brilliant Marketing

Dubai's government masterfully promotes the city's investment appeal. First, they create a unique project like the Burj Khalifa that captures worldwide attention. Then, they develop infrastructure for living and working around these landmarks.

The government's strategy can be described as:

  1. Attract attention with a unique project
  2. Create a comfortable living environment around it

The city is actively developing its infrastructure. According to the Dubai 2040 plan, parks and green spaces will increase by 105%. Nature parks and reserves will cover 60% of the emirate. 55% of residents will be able to reach a metro station within a 10-minute walk.

And that's not the only example. Dubai's roads surpass American and European standards — they're truly perfect. Another example: how authorities respond to natural disasters. After the abnormal rains in April 2024, the government immediately began constructing a new drainage system. They saw a problem and tackled it right away.

Soon, the city will see new major projects:

  • A monorail between Dubai and Abu Dhabi
  • The artificial island Palm Jebel Ali 

Palm Jebel Ali — the second palm island — is twice the size of The Palm Jumeirah. You can already buy property there, for example, in a prestigious Palm Jebel Ali Fond C project. If you're interested in learning more about the advantages and risks of investing in this area, contact Mira Real Estate experts.

3. Stable Economy

The best example of stability is the AED–USD exchange rate. Since 2010, it has barely changed, staying around 0.27. It's one of the world's most stable currencies, even more reliable than the euro. 

4. Political Neutrality

Since its founding, the UAE has been part of the non-aligned movement, following the principle of "absolute neutrality." This allows it to maintain equal distance from both West and East. This position has made the UAE a magnet for global elites, especially from the Middle East. For example, when political unrest began in Libya, many local investors moved to the UAE and invested in real estate to live in Dubai themselves and earn rental income. This helps sustain market price growth.
